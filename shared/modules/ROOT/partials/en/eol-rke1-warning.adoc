Rancher Kubernetes Engine (RKE/RKE1) reached End of Life (EOL) on July 31, 2025. Rancher 2.12.0 and later versions no longer support provisioning or managing downstream RKE1 clusters. We recommend replatforming your RKE1 clusters to RKE2 to to ensure continued support and security updates. For detailed migration guidance, review the https://www.suse.com/support/kb/doc/?id=000021518[SUSE Knowledge Base Article].
====
